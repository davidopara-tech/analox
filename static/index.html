About ANALOX

A terrestrial analog discovery platform focused on African sites for lunar and Martian research.

Scientific integrity

Scores produced by ANALOX are retrieval signals, not scientific findings. A candidate is never automatically classified as an analog. Findings require a human author and a review step. Every report prints a sample-data warning when mock values are present.

Consensus methodology

For each research area, criteria are min-max scaled across all candidates and the reference. L2 distance to the reference yields a similarity score per area. A weighted average across areas gives the total. Weights are configurable per query and never stored. The full breakdown is always returned — no black boxes.

Data provenance

Every measurement links to a source record with title, URL, and citation. Sources are preserved verbatim — they are not modified, merged, or inferred.

Built on

FastAPI · PostgreSQL · pgvector · SQLAlchemy · Pydantic. The Cochee numeric-vector similarity approach was adapted here for multi-perspective research consensus.
